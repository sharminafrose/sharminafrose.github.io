I am an R&D staff member at Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL), where I work on AI for quantum computing, machine learning for healthcare, and distributed quantum computing. I received my PhD in Computer Science and Application from Virginia Tech in 2022, advised by Danfeng (Daphne) Yao, and my BS in Computer Science and Engineering from Bangladesh University of Engineering and Technology (BUET). I was a postdoctoral research associate at ORNL from March 2023 to February 2026.

My current work includes:

- **Agentic AI for quantum computing.** I lead the Quantum Science Center's AI-for-quantum software work, including ChatQEC, which grounds every answer in cited literature so users can verify it, and OrcheQ, a multi-agent system for quantum circuit code generation, optimization, and quantum error correction.
- **Suicide predictive modeling.** I develop subgroup-aware, interpretable risk prediction methods for the US Department of Veterans Affairs (VA) population, with a focus on model fairness and bias across patient subgroups.
- **Distributed quantum computing.** I build an agentic MLIR-to-QIR translation pipeline with dual-backend verification and an end-to-end quantum circuit cutting pipeline, released under MACH-Q as Agentic-MLIR-to-QIR and Qweaver.

Earlier in my career I worked on security and privacy, including benchmarks and static analysis tools for detecting cryptographic API misuse in Java (CryptoAPI-Bench and CryptoGuard), local differential privacy for streaming IoT data, and bias correction for machine learning prognosis models on underrepresented patient groups.

I also mentor students at ORNL, and my recent honors include the IEEE Quantum Technical Community Distinguished Synergy Award (ORNL Quantum Team) and a winning-team selection in the IEEE Big Data 2026 Challenge on Explainable Suicide Risk Detection.
